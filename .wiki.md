# Project Summary
The Decentralized Land Registry Platform is a blockchain-based solution designed to address land ownership verification, registration, and dispute resolution. It aims to provide a transparent and tamper-proof system that empowers landowners and authorities, particularly in developing countries where traditional systems are often unreliable. By utilizing smart contracts, the platform automates processes such as ownership transfers and dispute resolutions, ensuring a fair and efficient mechanism for managing land-related conflicts.

# Project Module Description
- **Dashboard**: Overview of land records, disputes, and transfers.
- **Land Registry**: Management and registration of land parcels.
- **Ownership Transfer**: Secure transfer of land ownership using smart contracts.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Community-driven system for managing land disputes.
- **Smart Contract Interface**: View and interact with deployed smart contracts.
